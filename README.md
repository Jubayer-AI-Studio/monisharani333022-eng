### 💼 About Me

I am a **Software Engineer & AI Systems Architect** based in **Dhaka, Bangladesh**. I engineer scalable web platforms, full-stack SaaS architectures, and autonomous AI systems. My work focuses on bridging high-level AI capabilities (computer vision, voice biometric pipelines, agentic LLM workflows) with production-grade web infrastructure (Next.js 15, React 19, TypeScript, PostgreSQL, Drizzle ORM).

* 🚀 **Full-Stack SaaS Engineering:** Architect of [NoorHub](https://github.com/monisharani333022-eng/NoorHub-Madrasa-Management-SaaS) — an enterprise bilingual management platform featuring 29 completed routes, GIS spatial mapping of Bangladesh, and offline PWA capability.
* 🤖 **Autonomous AI Systems:** Creator of [Jubayer AI](https://github.com/monisharani333022-eng/Jubayer-Jarvis-AI-Automation) — a 100% hands-free personal desktop assistant with biometric voiceprint verification and real-time vision controls.
* ⚡ **High-Throughput Automation:** Designing resilient bot architectures with anti-detection heuristics, headless automation, and continuous delivery pipelines.
